# 2026 Jajucha Track Build Manual: English Digest

Digest of the Korean "2026 Hyundai Mobis Engineering Leader Festival Jajucha Competition Track Construction Guide" [2026 현대모비스 공학 리더 페스티벌 자주차 경진 대회 트랙 제작 안내], 8 PDF pages. Page 2 has no text layer and was read from its rendered image. Dimensions marked "(drawing)" come from labels on figures. Organizers advise schools to build their own track rather than buy one (track build manual, p. 1).

## 4. Starting light [스타팅 라이트]

School-built item (track build manual, p. 4).

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Parts | MAX7219 Arduino 8x32 dot-matrix module (red), starting-light signboard [표지판], light-shielding cover [빛 가림 덮개] made of box or cardboard | (track build manual, p. 4) |
| Behavior 1 | LED matrix lights up one segment at a time at 1.5 s intervals | (track build manual, p. 4) |
| Behavior 2 | After a random 3 to 6 s, all lights go out (start signal) | (track build manual, p. 4) |
| Sequence figure | Wait, then four stacked matrix modules fill red one by one from the bottom, then all off | (track build manual, p. 4) |
| Overall size (drawing) | About 160 mm tall x 80 mm wide | (track build manual, p. 4) |
| Cover side view (drawing) | Tapered trapezoid: 70 mm top, 130 mm height, 40 mm bottom; encloses dot matrix plus signboard | (track build manual, p. 4) |
| Placement | Placed at the roadside, small stand beside the lane, facing the car | (track build manual, p. 4) |

- The light-shielding cover is a shield [덮개] meant to keep ambient light off the matrix. The drawn trapezoid dimensions are my best reading of figure labels (track build manual, p. 4).
- The four lit steps correspond to a stack of four 8x8 sections of the 8x32 module (inferred from the figure; track build manual, p. 4).

## 7. Signs [표지판]

| Sign | Notes | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Starting-light sign | Portrait signboard attached to the starting-light stack | (track build manual, pp. 4, 7) |
| Pit sign [피트 표지판] | Low wide blue board reading PIT; used as the partition on an M panel | (track build manual, p. 7) |
| Yellow flag | Yellow portrait sign with a white flag | (track build manual, pp. 5, 7) |
| Sudden obstacle [돌발 장애물] | Cut-out of two running figures in orange suits | (track build manual, p. 7) |

- Make signs by printing on plain copier paper or matte-coated synthetic paper, then mounting on foam board or woodrock [우드락] (track build manual, p. 7).
- Sudden obstacle behavior: it is on the track before the car approaches and leaves the track a few seconds after the car arrives. Real competition moves it automatically; schools may move it by hand on a long pole (track build manual, p. 7).
- Print-ready sign image files are provided separately (track build manual, p. 7).
- Sign sizes are not given in the text. The only sized drawings are the starting light and speed meter (track build manual, pp. 4, 5, 7).

## 8. Purchase information

- Practice tracks can be bought through the textbook publisher's Naver store. Only the track and the hill are sold (track build manual, p. 8).
- Price: 990,000 KRW per set (track plus hill), including express delivery and VAT (track build manual, p. 8).
- Matte insulating tape [무광 절연 테이프] is not included; schools buy their own (track build manual, p. 8).
- Lead time one week; orders accepted until Friday, 23 October (track build manual, p. 8).

## 9. Known inconsistencies and gaps

- Inventory vs assembly for I and J: no mismatch found. The inventory says I 1 and J 5, and the assembly drawing has exactly one I (row 3, c11) and five J (r3 c10, r4 c3, r5 c3, r6 c1, r6 c2) (track build manual, pp. 1-2). If another source disagrees, verify against that source.
- Type E is absent from both inventory and assembly, so letters skip from D to F (track build manual, pp. 1-2).
- Page 1 title says the track is 41 panels, but the grid holds 66 cells; 25 cells are empty by design (track build manual, pp. 1-2).
- The assembly drawing shows no hill, tunnel, speed meter, starting light, pit or obstacle positions. Only the panel letters are drawn (track build manual, p. 2).
- The hill example photo is a perspective shot with no dimensions beyond the 900 x 900 x 100 mm block spec (track build manual, p. 3).
- Hill block cutting is only described as "triangular"; exact wedge geometry is not given (track build manual, p. 3).
- The tunnel top plate shows five 260 mm labels, while the title caption mentions only long 260 mm and short 98 mm sides (track build manual, p. 6).
- Some dimension labels on figures are small and were read visually; confirm 160/80 mm, 70/130/40 mm and 220/390 mm against the original PDF before relying on them (track build manual, pp. 4, 6).
