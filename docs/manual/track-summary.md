# 2026 Track Guide Summary (English digest)

Source: the Korean "2026 track guide summary" [2026 트랙 안내 요약 자료], 9 PDF pages. Pages 1 and 3 are drawings only; their labels were read from the images.

## Course overview

- The guide covers two runs: the qualifying run [예선 주행] and the final run [결선 주행]. (track summary, p. 2)
- The qualifying course map has 10 numbered steps and a yellow driving line with arrows. (track summary, p. 1)
- The final course map has 9 numbered steps, plus an extra mission box. (track summary, p. 3)
- Both maps mark the start/finish line [시작/종료선] at the bottom edge. (track summary, p. 1)
- Both maps place the starting light [스타팅 라이트] just left of the start/finish line. (track summary, p. 1)
- Both maps show a barrier [격벽] with the label "pit board" [피트 표지판] along the bottom right, near the finish. (track summary, p. 1)
- The final map uses the same start/finish, starting light and barrier positions as the qualifying map. (track summary, p. 3)

## Qualifying run steps [예선 주행 방법]

The qualifying run is a 10-step sequence: start on the starting-light signal; the S-curve; the right-angle section; the tunnel; two ramp steps (the second ends with a right turn and a straight); the zigzag section; the obstacle section, avoiding obstacles; a curved course followed by a straight; and a final drive to the finish line that must not touch the barrier. (track summary, p. 2)

## Final run steps [결선 주행 방법]

The final run has 9 steps plus an extra mission. After the same starting-light start, the order is: the S-shaped tunnel [S자 터널], the right-angle section, a U-turn then straight, the ㄱ-shaped ramp [ㄱ자 경사로], the zigzag section, the obstacle section, a curved course then straight, and the finish-line drive without touching the barrier. (track summary, p. 4)

- The extra mission pairs the yellow flag with speed measurement, and adds the sudden obstacle [옐로 플래그+속도 측정 / 돌발 장애물]. (track summary, p. 4)

## Missions on the qualifying course [예선 주행 코스]

### Mission 1: starting light [스타팅 라이트]

- A bank of four LEDs lights up in sequence; the moment all of them go dark together is the start signal. (track summary, p. 5)
- Timing: 1.5 s between lights; after the last one, the lights go out at a random moment 3 to 6 s later, and a long buzzer sounds with them. Leaving before the buzzer counts as a false start [부정 출발]. (track summary, p. 5)
- The drawing shows three phases: waiting, lights added one by one, then all out after the random delay. (track summary, p. 5)

### Mission 2: speed measurement [속도 측정] with yellow flag [옐로 플래그]

- When the flag appears, the car's speed over the following stretch is checked for slowing down, starting right after the flag; running above the set speed costs a penalty [페널티]. The flag's exact position and trigger time are announced on competition day. (track summary, p. 6)
- Speed comes from timing the car between two infrared sensors mounted 30 cm apart. The speed-meter drawing labels this gap 300 mm, which agrees. (track summary, p. 6)
- The drawing also labels 900 mm on a lower dashed arc; what it measures is not stated. (track summary, p. 6)

### Mission 3: sudden obstacle [돌발 장애물]

- Two runners in orange suits appear on the track. The car pauses and resumes once the obstacle is gone. (track summary, p. 7)
- Hitting the obstacle gives a penalty. (track summary, p. 7)

### Mission 4: ramp [경사로]

- Qualifying: the ramp is on a straight section of track. (track summary, p. 7)
- Final: the ramp is on a curved section of track. The final ramp area is labeled "hill section" [언덕 구간]. (track summary, p. 7)

### Mission 5: tunnel [터널]

- Qualifying: a U-shaped tunnel [ㄷ자 터널]. (track summary, p. 8)
- Final: an S-shaped tunnel [S자 터널]. (track summary, p. 8)

### Missions 6 and 7: right-angle corner [직각 코너] and obstacle avoidance [장애물 회피]

- The right-angle corner is the same as last year. Only its position has changed. (track summary, p. 8)
- Obstacle avoidance takes place in the obstacle section. The obstacle layout differs between qualifying and final, and will be announced later. (track summary, p. 8)

## Candidate sites for the extra mission

- Five candidate sites were chosen on straight sections on the outside of the track. (track summary, p. 9)
- The candidates are marked 1 to 5 on the map: 1 on the bottom straight left of the start line, 2 on the left vertical straight, 3 on the top straight, 4 on the right vertical straight, and 5 on the bottom straight right of the start line. (track summary, p. 9)
- The actual speed-measurement and sudden-obstacle positions will be disclosed on the competition day. (track summary, p. 9)

## Numbers shown in the guide

- Starting lights: 4 LEDs, 1.5 s between lights, 3 to 6 s random delay before the start. (track summary, p. 5)
- Speed sensors: 2 infrared sensors, 30 cm apart. (track summary, p. 6)
- Speed-meter drawing: 300 mm and 900 mm labels. (track summary, p. 6)
- Run steps: 10 in qualifying, 9 plus an extra mission in final. (track summary, p. 2; track summary, p. 4)
- Extra-mission candidate sites: 5. (track summary, p. 9)
